A free songbook for your lyrics and chords.

Campfire helps you organize your repertoire and keeps it ready for rehearsal and the stage, on whatever screen you play from.

Open in your browser Download Get the app Open in your browser

See all versions

  • Every platform

    Android, iOS, iPadOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, and the browser.

  • Works offline

    Songs are stored on your device, so a venue without signal is no problem.

  • Uncluttered interface

    Nothing on screen but the song. Light, dark, or sepia, in the accent color you pick.

  • No ads, no account

    No tracking, subscription, in-app purchases, or song limits either.

Columns that follow your text size

Set the text as large as you need to read it from the stand, and the song is laid out in as many columns as fit at that size.

  • Turn pages with a tap or a page-turner pedal.
  • Chord diagrams sit at the top of the song.
  • Auto-scroll plays the song in one column, timed to its length, with a speed you can adjust.
  • A lyrics-only mode hides the chords for singers.
Use the slider to change the text size

Import and edit

Paste a song copied from any website, however messy, and it opens in the editor with the chords lined up over the lyrics and the verses and choruses picked out.

  • Import text files, PDFs, Word documents, zip archives, and backups from other apps.
  • The editor points out mistakes and shows a live preview as you type.
  • Look up a song’s album, year, songwriters, and cover art online.

Setlists and sharing

Play through a set in order, with the next song always one tap away.

  • A setlist stores the key, capo, and tempo of each of its songs.
  • The app counts down to the date of the gig and adds up the running time.
  • Print songs or the whole set from a PDF, with the running order.
  • Send a set to other Campfire users as a zip file, or as a link with a QR code they scan to open it.

Metronome and tuner

Each song stores its tempo, time signature, and accented beats, so the click starts from the song’s screen with one tap.

  • In a set, the tempo switches to the next song’s on the next bar.
  • Tempo changes written inside a song are followed too.
  • The tuner opens over the song you’re about to play and ticks off each string once it’s in tune.
  • The sound is analyzed on your device and never recorded.

Chords and scales

Pick your instrument once, and every song’s chord diagrams are drawn for it: guitar, ukulele, mandolin, banjo, or keyboard.

  • Diagrams and tabs follow the tuning you play in, from drop D to open G.
  • Search the chord database by key or name, and see recommended scales for each chord.
  • The Scales tab draws every scale and mode in any key, from pentatonic and blues to bebop, symmetric, and world scales, on any fretboard with any tuning, or on the keyboard.

Notation

Show chords as letters, German, Latin, Nashville numbers, or Roman numerals, in any key. Melodies written in ABC notation are drawn as sheet music, and tabs move their frets with the key. Underneath, every song is a plain ChordPro text file, so nothing is locked in. Try it on the example below.

What else is in it

Everything a songbook needs, and nothing that gets in your way.

  • Search and filter

    Search by title, artist, or tag, with the closest matches first, and filter by language. Select many songs at once to add them to a setlist or share them.

  • Tags

    Give songs your own tags in 14 colors, many at once, and list the songs that carry any or all of the ones you pick. Tags are saved in the song files.

  • Sharing

    Send songs and setlists to the musicians you play with, as a zip file or a link any Campfire user can open.

  • Real-time Dropbox sync

    Edit a song on your laptop and see it change on your phone a moment later, with everything stored in your own Dropbox folder.

  • Read-only mode

    A simple toggle locks editing on stage, so a stray tap can’t change a song or a setlist in the middle of a set.

  • Hide what you don’t use

    Turn off the features you don’t need, and Campfire hides them to keep your experience simple and free of distractions.
