Your songbook, on every screen you own.

Campfire keeps your lyrics and chords as plain text files and turns them into pages you can play from: on your phone, your tablet, your computer or in the browser. It’s free and open source, and it doesn’t ask you to make an account.

Songs are text files you can read without Campfire.

Every song is a ChordPro file: the lyrics, with the chords written in brackets where they fall. Most chord sites and songbook apps can export it, so bring what you already have, or write new songs in the editor with a live preview next to it. Try the transpose buttons below.

Made to be read from a music stand.

Songs spread out into as many columns as the screen can fit, and can carry on to the right instead of downwards, so scrolling never takes you back up in the middle of a verse.

Transpose
Move a song to your key without touching the file. Campfire remembers it for the next time.
Text size
Bigger or smaller right on the song, for the phone on your knee or the tablet on a stand.
Lyrics only
Hide the chords when you hand the screen to the singers.
Setlists
Put songs in order for the evening, with a note about what the set is for.
Tabs and grids
Picking patterns and chord grids are drawn the way you wrote them.
Performance mode
Locks the app against accidental edits while you’re playing.

Your library stays with you.

Campfire works offline, and there’s no Campfire account or server. Your songs are kept on your device, and you can export the whole library as a zip whenever you like.

To have the same songs on your phone and your laptop, connect your own Dropbox. Campfire can only see its own folder there, and the files go straight between your devices and Dropbox. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking; the privacy policy has the details.
