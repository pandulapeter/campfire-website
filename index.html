Laid out for the way you play.

Campfire fits each song into as many columns as your screen has room for. Turn the page with a tap, or hands-free with a page-turner pedal. Chord diagrams sit at the top, for guitar, ukulele, or keyboard, in the shapes you play.

Switch to lyrics only for the singers, with your links to the original recording right there with the song.

Setlists, on screen or on paper.

Put songs in order and play through the whole set without stopping. A setlist remembers the key, capo, and tempo of each of its songs, counts down to the date of the gig, and adds up its running time.

Export a song or the whole set as a PDF to print a copy for your music stand. Effortlessly share entire setlists with other Campfire users.

A metronome that knows each song.

Every song keeps its own tempo and time signature, so one tap starts the click right where the song needs it. The app seamlessly transitions from one BPM to another, whether across the setlist or within the same song.

The metronome has tap tempo, accents, subdivisions, five sounds to choose from, and it keeps going with the screen locked.

Bring your songs, or write them from scratch.

Import text files, PDFs, Word documents, a whole zip, or a backup from another app. Campfire will do its best to create songs you can transpose.

Writing your own is just as easy, with an editor that catches mistakes and shows the result as you type.

Chords the way you read them.

Letters, German, Latin, Nashville numbers, or Roman numerals, in any key, and tabs transpose right along with the chords. Underneath, every song is a plain ChordPro text file, so nothing is locked in.

# Raw .cho file
{title: House of the Rising Sun}
{artist: Traditional American}

{start_of_verse}
There [Am]is a [C]house in [D]New Or[F]leans

{end_of_verse}

What else is in it

Everything a songbook needs, and nothing that gets in your way.

  • Search and filter

    Search by title, artist, or tag, with the closest matches first. Every song shows its key and tempo before you open it.

  • Tags

    Make your own labels for genres, moods, or songs to practice, and narrow the list down by them.

  • Dropbox sync

    Connect your own Dropbox and your songs and setlists stay the same on every device.

  • Read only mode

    Locks editing while you play, so a stray tap can’t change a song mid-set.

  • Only what you use

    Switch off the metronome, setlists, chords, or cover art, and the app gets simpler still.

  • Sharing

    Send songs and setlists to the musicians you play with.
