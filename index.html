Your songbook, on every screen you own.

Campfire keeps your lyrics and chords in one place and arranges each song to fit the screen it’s on, whether that’s the phone in your pocket, the tablet on your music stand or the laptop at rehearsal.

Open in your browser Download

  • On all your devices

    Phones, tablets, computers and the browser, all sharing one library.

  • Works offline

    Songs are stored on your device, so no signal is needed to play.

  • Clean and modern

    An uncluttered interface in light or dark, in the color you pick.

  • Free and open source

    No ads, tracking or account, and the source code is on GitHub.

Made to be read from a music stand.

The number of columns follows your screen and text size: one on a phone, several on a tablet or a laptop.

Longer songs carry on to the right instead of downwards, so scrolling never takes you back up in the middle of a verse.

And when you hand the screen to the singers, the chords can stay hidden.

Bring the songs you already have.

Campfire uses ChordPro, the format most chord sites and songbook apps can export: the lyrics, with the chords in brackets where they fall.
