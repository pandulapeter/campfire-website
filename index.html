MADE FOR THE MOMENT

Everything you need.
Room to make it yours.

01 / YOUR LIBRARY

A songbook that stays yours.

Write and edit lyrics and chords, import your ChordPro files, and organize songs into setlists. Export your library whenever you like.

02 / YOUR WAY TO PLAY

Find your key. Take the stage.

Transpose on the fly, adjust the text size, or switch to lyrics only. Performance mode keeps accidental edits out of your set.

03 / YOUR OWN SPACE

Just you and the music.

No ads, no tracking, no Campfire account. Keep your library on your device, with optional sync through your own Dropbox.
